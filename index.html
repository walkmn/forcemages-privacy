Force Mages: Wizard Brawl — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Force Mages: Wizard Brawl ("the game") is published by Andrii Tsymbal, Ukraine ("we"). Contact: ognarik@gmail.com

This policy is published at https://walkmn.github.io/forcemages-privacy/

What the game stores on your device

  • Your game progress: trophies, coins, gems, mages and their levels, chests, quests, season pass progress, and the nickname you chose.
  • Your settings: sound and music on or off.

This is kept on your device only. We have no player accounts and no copy of your progress on our servers. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it.

What is sent to others

The game has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. It uses the following services, which receive some information directly from your device.

Adverts — Unity LevelPlay (Unity Ads and ironSource Ads)

Adverts are shown only when you choose to watch one for a reward. To choose, show and measure adverts, the advertising providers may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and system version, and information about the adverts you saw or tapped.

Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Purchases — Google Play Billing

Purchases are handled by Google. The game receives confirmation that a purchase was made and what was bought. We never see your card or other payment details.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics and crash reports — Unity Analytics and Unity Cloud Diagnostics

To balance the game and fix errors we record events such as matches played, their results and length, chests opened, upgrades bought, adverts watched, purchases made, and crashes with technical details of the device (model, system version, game version). These are tied to an anonymous identifier created for the game, not to your name or email address.

Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, write to us and we will help to have it removed.

Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising identifier or switch off personalised advertising in your device's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Watching adverts and making purchases is always your choice; the game can be played without either.
  • Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored on the device.
  • To ask about, or to request deletion of, data collected by the services above, write to ognarik@gmail.com. We will pass the request on to the provider where the data is held by them.

Changes

We will post changes to this policy at https://walkmn.github.io/forcemages-privacy/ and update the date above.
